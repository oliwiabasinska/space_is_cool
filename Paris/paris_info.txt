Paris is getting hotter: A changing summer

For decades, Paris has been known for its beautiful summer days, when
people could relax along the Seine and enjoy the sunshine.

However, temperatures in Paris have been rising over time. Our
temperature data from 1981 to 2026 shows an increase in the highest
monthly temperatures. The trend line indicates an increase of
approximately 0.54°C per decade.

This change has become particularly noticeable in recent years. During
summer 2026, Paris experienced several extreme heatwaves. On 24 June,
temperatures reached around 40°C in parts of the city, and July became
the hottest July ever recorded in Paris.
